# Palette provenance

Palette Engine contains 39 original project palettes under stable identifiers `PE001` through `PE039`.

## Collection

- `PE001`–`PE022` and `PE036`–`PE039`: combinations curated and supplied by the repository owner in 2026.
- `PE023`–`PE035`: combinations developed specifically for Palette Engine in 2026 and selected for inclusion by the repository owner.

The collection contains 17 two-color palettes, 11 three-color palettes, six four-color palettes, and five five-color palettes.

No palette data transcribed from third-party books or commercial palette collections is distributed in this repository. Earlier private research material is not part of this public project or its Git history.

## Color values

Hex and sRGB values are the primary definitions. CMYK values are calculated automatically from sRGB with a simple mathematical conversion and are included only as approximate conveniences. They are not color-managed conversions for a particular ICC profile, press, ink, or paper.
